Why Choose Fairway Pavilion

The Fairway Pavilion is a peaceful purpose built wedding and event venue nestled within 120 acres of The West Middlesex Golf Club. It is situated in a secure, secluded location in west London between Ealing, Greenford and Southall. Fairway Pavilion offers an exceptional banqueting experience for weddings, receptions, and grand celebrations. Our elegant venue combines modern luxury with traditional charm, perfect for hosting memorable events.
